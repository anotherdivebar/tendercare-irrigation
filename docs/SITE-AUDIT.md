# Reference audit

Inspected the supplied Webflow site on September 4, 2026 through its rendered page, navigation, accessibility tree and imagery.

Reference: https://tendercare-irrigation.webflow.io/

## What the existing site contains

- TenderCare logo with burgundy lettering; green estimate buttons; pale gray surfaces and oversized sans-serif headings.
- Header: Services mega-menu, About, Blog, Support menu (FAQs/Contact), estimate CTA.
- Nine services grouped into irrigation, drainage and smart/seasonal care: installation, repair, maintenance, yard drainage, French drains, downspout extensions, controllers, backflow, winterization.
- Main pitch: comprehensive irrigation for unpredictable Kansas weather; installation through maintenance, certification and winterization.
- A local-professional trust section with Hunter branding. No supporting proof of partnership or certification was provided.
- Four packages: Gold City Water, Gold Well Water, Silver City Water, Silver Well Water. No dollar amounts were visible.
- Gold City Water: startup, backflow certification, two mid-season evaluations, winterization. Gold Well Water omits backflow certification. Silver lists one mid-season evaluation instead of two, with the same city/well distinction.
- Source footnote: up to 12 zones and one PVB device; more devices/controllers/zones may incur added charges. Another note promises Gold customers 15% off parts and a 72-hour repair turnaround. Those commercial promises require client confirmation and were not republished.
- Gallery, six testimonials, final lawn-health CTA, four FAQs and contact section.
- Local positioning: Wichita metro, with Derby, Andover, Maize, Haysville and Goddard mentioned.

## Problems found

All 41 inspected destination links pointed to `#`, including navigation, service links, estimate buttons, phone, email, address and social profiles. The site is a shell rather than a functional conversion journey.

The hero heading clipped horizontally in the inspected desktop viewport. The visual system relies on oversized text and repeated template blocks, with limited hierarchy between actual services.

The hero alt text describes a school campus; testimonial assets include agriculture, plumbing, a campus and moving imagery. The six names and generic quotations have no supplied verification. Pricing feature lists contain unrelated template language such as “everything in basic/business plus” and differ from the clear package summaries.

Phone `(316) 555-1234` is a placeholder. The email, Derby address, stated hours and quick-response claims remain unverified. The footer copyright is incomplete. All social destinations are placeholders.

## Rebuild decisions

Preserve the legitimate service hierarchy. The source package structure was initially documented but was later removed from the rebuild at the client’s direction. Replace disconnected template content with property-focused explanations, real routes, descriptive internal links, answer-first FAQs and a minimal inquiry flow.

Use warm ivory, deep green, subtle burgundy, restrained rules and a consistent architectural grid. Vary composition with service directory rows, image/text features, field photography and structured editorial content.

Use the 15 user-supplied photos from `F:\TenderCare Irrigation`. The preview uses irrigation and fieldwork images with literal alt descriptions; no made-up locations, dates, clients, completion statistics or project results. The available images do not establish a matched before/after pair or a completed drainage project. Drainage pages use general fieldwork imagery and do not claim that the pictured irrigation work is a drainage installation.

The reference logo was downloaded from the reference site’s public asset URL. Confirm approved master artwork and image usage rights before public launch. Truck photos show an older-looking number; that is recorded as a lead for verification, not used as the current phone.
